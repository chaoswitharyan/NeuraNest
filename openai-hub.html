INTRODUCTION

What is OpenAI?

OpenAI is an artificial intelligence research and technology company. It develops AI models and tools that help people learn, create, solve problems and build software applications.

Its technologies include ChatGPT and developer tools that allow programmers to build AI-powered experiences.

CORE TECHNOLOGIES

Explore OpenAI Technologies

ChatGPT

A conversational AI assistant that can answer questions, explain concepts, help with writing and support coding tasks.

GPT Models

AI models designed to understand instructions, process information and generate useful responses across different tasks.

Multimodal AI

AI systems that can work with different types of information, such as text, images and audio.

OpenAI API

Developer tools that allow programmers to integrate AI capabilities into their own websites and applications.

AI Agents

AI systems that can work through multi-step tasks and use connected tools to help achieve a particular goal.

AI Safety

Research and practices focused on making artificial intelligence more reliable, secure and beneficial.

BEYOND TEXT

Understanding Multimodal AI

Traditional computer programs often work with one specific type of input. Multimodal AI can process and work with multiple types of information.

Text

Understands questions, instructions, conversations and written information.

Images

Can interpret visual information such as photographs, diagrams and screenshots.

Audio

Can support speech-related tasks such as understanding spoken input and generating spoken responses.

BUILDING RESPONSIBLY

Why Does AI Safety Matter?

As AI becomes more capable, it is important to consider accuracy, privacy, fairness, security and the possible effects of AI-generated information.

AI systems can make mistakes, so important outputs should be checked rather than accepted blindly.
