What is NVIDIA?

NVIDIA is a technology company known for its GPUs and computing platforms. Its hardware and software help researchers and developers build, train and run artificial intelligence systems.

Important NVIDIA AI Technologies

NVIDIA Nemotron

A family of AI models designed for language tasks, reasoning and building AI applications.

NVIDIA NeMo

A framework and collection of tools for developing, customizing and managing generative AI models.

NVIDIA NIM

A set of inference microservices that helps developers deploy AI models through standardized interfaces.

Physical AI

AI systems that interact with the physical world, including robotics, simulation and autonomous machines.

Why NVIDIA Matters in AI

NVIDIA provides computing hardware, software libraries and development platforms that support AI research and real-world applications across industries.
