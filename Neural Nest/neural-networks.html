Inside a Neural Network

Neural networks are computing systems that learn patterns from examples and use those patterns to make predictions.

How Does It Work?

Information passes through connected layers of artificial neurons.

📥

1. Input Layer

Receives the information, such as image pixels, numbers, or other data.

⚙️

2. Hidden Layers

Process information using connections, weights, and mathematical functions to learn patterns.

📤

3. Output Layer

Produces a result, such as a prediction, classification, or generated output.
