AI BEYOND THE CLASSROOM

AI in the Real World

Artificial intelligence is used across industries to assist people, analyze information, and solve complex problems.

Where Can We Find AI?

🏥

Healthcare

AI can assist medical professionals with analyzing scans, organizing information, and supporting research.

🎓

Education

Learning platforms can use AI to explain concepts, provide feedback, and adapt learning materials.

🚗

Transportation

AI can help with route planning, traffic prediction, and driver-assistance technologies.

🏦

Banking

Financial institutions use AI to detect unusual transactions and identify potential fraud.

🛍️

Online Shopping

Recommendation systems suggest products based on browsing patterns and customer preferences.

🌾

Agriculture

AI can help analyze crop images, monitor conditions, and support agricultural planning.

AI and Responsible Innovation

AI can be useful, but it also raises concerns about privacy, bias, security, and accuracy. Human judgment and responsible use remain important.
