INTRODUCTION

What are AI Agents?

AI agents are software systems designed to work towards a goal. Depending on their design, they can understand instructions, plan steps, use available tools and evaluate the results of their actions.

Unlike a basic chatbot that primarily responds to messages, an AI agent can be designed to carry out a sequence of actions.

THE PROCESS

How Do AI Agents Work?

01

Understand

The agent receives a goal or instruction from the user.

02

Plan

It breaks the goal into smaller steps and decides what to do.

03

Act

It uses available tools and resources to perform tasks.

04

Evaluate

It checks the results and determines whether more work is needed.

AGENT ARCHITECTURE

Types of AI Agents

Simple Reflex Agents

Respond to situations using predefined condition-and-action rules.

Goal-Based Agents

Select actions based on a goal they are trying to achieve.

Learning Agents

Improve their behaviour using information gained from experience or feedback.

Multi-Agent Systems

Multiple agents interact or collaborate to work on a shared or separate set of goals.

RESPONSIBLE DEVELOPMENT

Challenges and Limitations

AI agents can make incorrect decisions, misunderstand instructions or misuse tools when poorly designed.

Important considerations include data privacy, security, human supervision, reliability and permission before taking significant actions.
